# Concept rationales

One page per direction. All four share the same verified content (three FilmTwist case studies, five provisional services,
one consultation CTA, FilmTwist attribution) and share nothing else: no section template, no type pairing, no palette
treatment, no animation system.

---

## 01 Clean & Modern: A Clearer Path to Enrollment

**Role.** The direction closest to what the client described: the clarity of the Canadian and Bloom references, with far
stronger work thumbnails.

**Thesis.** Competence should be obvious before anyone reads a sentence. The design gets out of the way of the work.

**How it shows up.** White canvas, generous whitespace, brand blue and green as the only accents, Josefin Sans (the brand
face) for headlines over Inter for body. An asymmetric hero: assertive headline left, one oversized art-directed campaign
still right. Three case compositions that deliberately break the grid (8/4, then 4/7 with a square crop, then a 9/3 wide
band), numbered 01/02/03, each with one correctly labeled metric and its period. Services as five cards with hand-drawn SVG
glyphs. The four-step approach as an elegant timeline. Motion is restricted to a hero load-in and hover/focus states; it is
complete with motion off.

**Strength.** Fastest comprehension; lowest production risk; strongest on a phone.
**Tradeoff.** Least surprising. If the client wants the site itself to demonstrate creative range, this concept proves it
through the thumbnails only.

## 02 Bold & Creative: Creative That Fills Classrooms

**Role.** Demonstrate FilmTwist's production and design quality before explaining the service stack.

**Thesis.** The work is the argument. Visitors should think "these people make excellent things" within a second, and
still understand the enrollment offer within five.

**How it shows up.** An ink-black canvas with electric green and teal accents; Archivo at wide, heavy weights for display
against Josefin Sans labels. A hero built from layered real campaign assets (Friendship's square ads, Aspire's social
mockups, Envision's billboard frames, school photography) with word-by-word kinetic type and a gentle cursor tilt on
desktop. A pausable marquee of campaign thumbnails with real captions. A portfolio with working category filters (All,
Enrollment campaigns, Video & photo, Digital ads, Print & out-of-home) over 26 real items, each opening an inline
expansion overlay with the case story, metric, period and source link. Services as a "what we make / what it does" ledger.
Proof as oversized numerals with the historical period attached.

**Strength.** Proves creative capability fastest; the most "agency" of the four.
**Tradeoff.** Heaviest page and busiest first screen; every image on it needs client approval before launch.

## 03 The Invisible School

**Role.** The single heavily animated alternative: memorable, friendly, clever, with a real service site beneath.

**Thesis.** Many excellent schools are simply under-discovered. Visibility, not quality, is what changes.

**How it shows up.** The page opens on the official logo with the tagline "grow your own." set as live text beneath it. On
scroll the period pops free, drops into the world, squashes on landing and becomes a small character with a shadow. A pinned,
side-scrolling town drawn as layered paper cut-outs (sky, far rooftops, a street of houses and shops, the school, foreground
lamps and bushes at five parallax depths) begins in a muted dusk palette and warms into brand color as one continuous tween.
The five beats follow FilmTwist's published K-12 recruitment funnel: a blank sign comes alive; digital ads (a social video on a
phone, a search result, a streaming spot, a shelter poster) light up along main street and the first families appear; a
landing page card opens, families walk in from both sides, bunting and window light fill the school, and an email nudges the
one family that hesitated; the school is full, seen and chosen. Captions are editorial type set in the sky with a short
subline, not boxes. The character arcs back to its place as the period, the logo reconstitutes and the CTA appears. Then a
conventional, readable site: three case studies, services, the four-step funnel, CTA, footer. "Skip the story" at the top,
"Rewatch" after it, and a static four-frame version (same world, same palette journey) under reduced motion. Josefin Sans
with Nunito for a rounded, friendly body.

Revised October 27, 2026 after Noah's review of the first build ("the scroll animation is not professionally done"): the clip-art
town, the saturation-blend desaturation, the 2.8x raster logo zoom, the tooltip-style caption boxes and the marble "families"
were all replaced.

**Strength.** The one people will remember and talk about at the conference.
**Tradeoff.** Longest path to the services; the most engineering to maintain; must be tested on real iOS devices.

## 04 Start With Your Number (invented direction, second version)

**Role.** A different communication archetype: the page is a worksheet, and the visitor's own school is its subject.
Replaces "The Enrollment Year" (season dial), which Noah rejected on October 27, 2026 as unprofessional with an unclear
architecture. The replacement was designed from research first; see `CONCEPT_04_RESEARCH.md`.

**Thesis.** Every agency page in this category starts with the agency. The research on how school leaders are told to
choose says the opposite: ask for the funnel numbers first, and judge on seats filled and what each cost. So this page
opens with the one number a leader already knows, the seats to fill, and works everything out from it, using only the
three campaigns FilmTwist can show.

**How it shows up.** A dark worksheet hero: one input (seats), one adjustable assumption (the application-to-enrollment
yield, preset to the one campaign that reported confirmed enrollments), and four computed rows: seats, applications
needed, inquiries needed as a range with the three campaigns plotted, and the media budget those campaigns' cost per lead
implies, also as a range. Figures ease to new values. Then proof as data: the three campaigns drawn as funnels (leads,
applications, confirmed enrollments or "not reported") with period, cost per lead and source. Then the funnel workflow,
each step labeled with the worksheet row it moves. Then "what we ask on the first call": the list of numbers to bring.
Services, invitation ("Bring your number. We'll bring ours."), footer. Space Grotesk for words, JetBrains Mono for
figures, FSM blue ink with the brand green reserved for computed numbers and the CTA.

**Why it is fundamentally different.** It is the only concept where the reader does something other than read; the only
one that leads with the reader's school instead of the agency; the only one whose proof section is a comparison of
funnels rather than cards; and the only one that tells the visitor what the discovery call will ask of them.

**Strength.** Credibility through arithmetic on sourced figures; one slider at a conference booth leaves the visitor with
a number. **Tradeoff.** The ranges are wide because three campaigns are three campaigns; the client must be comfortable
with their published figures being used this way (listed in `CONTENT_AND_CLAIMS.md`).
