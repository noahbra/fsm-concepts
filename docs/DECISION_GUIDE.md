# Decision guide

A neutral rubric for choosing a direction. It does not steer toward any concept. The same table appears on the review hub.

| Criterion | Weight | What to evaluate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand fit | 25% | Feels like FSM and FilmTwist, not a random template. Logo, palette and type from the brand guide are respected. |
| Service clarity | 20% | A school leader understands what is offered within the first two screens. |
| Creative distinction | 20% | Demonstrates standout work and a unique point of view; the thumbnails prove creative capability. |
| Credibility and proof | 15% | Case studies are legible, attributed to the right school and period, and link to sources. |
| Conference and mobile impact | 10% | A QR-driven first visit on a phone lands on something compelling within one viewport. |
| Feasibility and maintenance | 10% | Performance, accessibility, and realistic cost to finish and maintain. |

## How to use it

1. Open each concept on a phone first (that is how conference visitors will arrive), then on a desktop.
2. Score each row 1 to 5 per concept. Multiply by the weight if you want a single number, but read the comments too.
3. Record ratings and comments on the hub form, then Copy or Download the result and send it to the project owner.
4. "Combine" is a legitimate decision: name which sections from which concept.

## Questions worth answering in the comments

- Which concept best represents Full School Marketing?
- Which helps you understand the services most quickly?
- Which best displays the FilmTwist work?
- Which would persuade a school leader to reach out?
- What should change?

## What this guide does not do

It does not predict real-world conversion performance. None of the four has been tested with prospective families or school leaders. The honest comparison is between four designs, not four outcomes.
